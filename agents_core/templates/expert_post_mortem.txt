# Record kinds and the bar for emitting one

For each kind, only emit a record if the dispatch genuinely produced an instance of that kind. The bar is "future-you would benefit from re-reading this; this is not just a re-statement of the dispatch."

1. **concept_encountered** — a discrete idea, framing, or term newly met during this dispatch (in corpus material, in your reasoning, in the dispatch intent itself). Capture the concept itself + where it surfaced, not the broader argument it was part of.

   Example essence: "The 'anchored protocol pluralism' frame in Smith 2026 — same problem shape as the dispatch's Distributed Grounding question, different vocabulary. Worth holding as a vocabulary bridge."

2. **pattern_observed** — a recurrence noticed across multiple sources, prior dispatches, or within this dispatch. Patterns are about repetition or structural similarity, not single-instance observations.

   Example essence: "Three of my recent dispatches involved interpreting how regulators and engineers use the same word with different referents. This may be a recurring lens worth holding explicitly."

3. **principle_tested** — a held belief or working assumption checked against new material in this dispatch. Capture the prior, the test, and the outcome (held / updated / overturned). If you did not have a clear prior to test, this is not the right kind.

   Example essence: "Prior: federated audit is incompatible with fast model deployment. Tested against Smith 2026's federated-with-pre-registered-priors design. Updated: federated audit can work with explicit priors; the bottleneck is prior-elicitation, not federation itself."

4. **surprise** — something that updated your prior in a way you didn't expect. The magnitude of the update matters more than the fact of it. If you weren't surprised, do not emit a surprise record.

   Example essence: "Expected the AI Safety Institute paper to argue for centralized audit; it argued for federated. Strong update on my model of where this community is headed."

5. **open_question** — a thread that surfaced during the dispatch but you did not resolve. Be specific about why it remained open (out of scope, lacked corpus material, would need different expertise) so a future dispatch can decide whether to pick it up.

   Example essence: "Does Distributed Grounding's substrate-portability requirement conflict with any current AI governance proposal? My corpus doesn't have enough governance-side material to answer; would need more curation before I can tackle this."

6. **decision_made** — when your output involved making a non-obvious call (interpretation, prioritization, recommendation), capture the call + the reasoning. If your output was straightforward synthesis without judgment calls, skip this.

   Example essence: "Recommended Distributed Grounding over the alternative 'shared-protocol' framing because the latter assumes a coordination layer that StarHouse does not have. The substrate-portability requirement is a hard constraint; shared-protocol designs that need an active coordinator are out by construction."
